# Design update

Updates a site that is already installed: pages, design and the application version.
The client's products, services, enquiries, orders, contact details and accounts are kept.

- **Linux:** `sudo ./update.sh` (or `sudo ./update.sh /path/to/installed/site`)
- **Windows:** double-click `update.cmd`

The installed site is found automatically. At the end the address and ports are shown.
